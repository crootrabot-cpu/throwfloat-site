The deploy

One accurate throw. Automatic rescue.

The ball flies true because it's a football. When it hits water the sensor fires the inflator. Seconds later it's a stable, high-buoyancy float the person can climb onto or hold.

How it works

Throw. Contact. Inflate.

  1. Throw like normal — familiar spiral, good distance even for kids or untrained rescuers.
  2. Water sensor triggers — built-in mechanism releases compressed gas or chemical reaction.
  3. Rapid inflation — ball transforms into a bright orange horseshoe float with reflective tape and grab handles.
